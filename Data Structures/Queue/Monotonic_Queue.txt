Monotonic Queue :
A monotonic queue is a queue where elements are always maintained in either increasing or decreasing order from front to rear.

Two Types:
Monotonic Increasing has Order : front → rear (small to big) and Front holds Minimum element.
Monotonic Decreasing has Order : front → rear (big to small) and Front holds Maximum element.

We can have either a monotonic queue , deque , circular queue.
And it is implemented using the original queues , deques , circular queues so its not a new Data Structure but just a Comparison Rule.
It is helpful in solving Sliding Window Max Questions.
